About Tidewell

We walk cold northern shorelines at low tide and pick up what the water has already finished working on. Nothing is quarried, blasted or machine-tumbled. Stones are rinsed in fresh water, air dried, sorted by hand, and sent out in recycled packaging.

We gather only where local collecting is permitted, and we take a little from many beaches rather than a lot from one.

Questions

How much is a kilo of stones?

Roughly a large handful to two handfuls, depending on stone size. Pebble grades give many more pieces per kilo than the large flats.

Are they aquarium safe?

Our basalt and granite grades are inert and aquarium safe. Rinse before use. Avoid the chalk-white grade in soft-water tanks.

Will my stones look like the photos?

Close, but not identical. Natural stone varies in colour and shape — that's the point of it.

Do they stay shiny?

Wet stones look darker and glossier. Dry, they go matte. A drop of mineral oil brings the colour back if you want it.
